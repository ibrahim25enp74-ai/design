# Skylight 6 x 15 m – shot prompts

Use each render as the reference / init image (img2img, ControlNet depth or "image prompt"),
then paste the prompt. Common settings: photorealistic, 16:9, keep the geometry.

Common negative prompt:
`clear glass, white roof, curved arch, trusses, extra columns, people, cars, text, watermark, cartoon, low poly, distorted perspective`

---

## 01 – لقطة جوية مائلة (shot_01_aerial.png)
Photorealistic aerial 3/4 view of a modern steel skylight roof spanning a narrow courtyard between two
traditional Iraqi concrete houses in Mosul, low gable roof of black smoked multiwall polycarbonate panels on a
square grid of slim dark-grey 80 mm steel tubes, 11 portal frames, row of black-glass ventilation windows along
both long sides, steel legs standing on the flat roofs behind beige plastered parapets, warm afternoon sun,
soft shadows, clear blue sky, architectural photography, 35 mm lens, high detail, 8k

## 02 – من الحوش للأعلى (shot_02_courtyard_looking_up.png)
Photorealistic interior view from the floor of a narrow residential courtyard looking up at a black smoked
polycarbonate skylight roof, dark steel square-tube grid of 75 x 67 cm cells, shallow pitched ridge with short
collar ties, filtered dim daylight casting a soft grid of shadows on cream plastered walls, stone tiled floor,
calm moody atmosphere, Iraqi house, wide-angle 18 mm, architectural interior photography, realistic materials

## 03 – من سطح البيت على الشبابيك (shot_03_rooftop_side_windows.png)
Eye-level photograph taken on a flat Iraqi house rooftop looking along a 15 m long steel skylight edge, a
continuous band of aluminium-framed black-glass top-hung ventilation windows between slim dark steel legs, legs
on small steel base plates on the roof slab, low beige parapet with overhanging coping in front, black
polycarbonate roof above, bright midday sun, crisp shadows, realistic concrete texture, 24 mm lens, 8k

## 04 – واجهة طرفية (shot_04_end_view.png)
