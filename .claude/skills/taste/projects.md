## Al-Yarmouk Commercial Souq (سوق اليرموك التجاري)

The drawing set in this repo: `SOUQ_A3_SHEET_SET.dxf`, plotted to `SOUQ_A3_SHEET_SET.pdf` by `plot_sheet_set.py`.

### Facts

- **Identity**: project PRJ-2026-002 in Mosul, Iraq, designed by Ibrahim Mahfoudh. Phase: design development, for review. First issue 30/09/2026.
- **Site**: plot 432/20 & 3/15, district 11 Wadi Al-Ain North (مقاطعة 11 وادي العين الشمالية), 25,060 m². It sits next to the Abu Tammam overpass (مجسر أبي تمام) and Al-Yabisat street (شارع اليابسات). The aerial on sheet 01 shows a dense, low, beige city fabric around the site.
- **Program**:
  - 158 shop modules (10,032 m² GF) along 8.00 m pedestrian souq corridors, as double rows of 20.00 m back-to-back shops.
  - A multi-storey building with a 1,044 m² floor plate: a store/mall on the ground and first floors, office apartments on the typical floors.
  - Prayer hall and ablution (199 m²) with a mashrabiya screen between the men's and women's halls. Qibla axis 191°, still to be verified.
  - Two public WC blocks.
- **Parking**: 339 cars in total. A 192-car basement at −3.50 and 147 surface spaces.
- **Open space**: a central pergola courtyard plaza (70.53 × 21.39 m) and a fountain court (20 × 20 m). Elements: limestone paving with a star inlay, a water rill with jets, a star fountain, date palms in raised planters on a 12 m grid, curved benches, café sets, lanterns.
- **Shop models**: an interior shop and an exterior double-front shop. Roof at +6.40, parapet at +8.00. PV panels tilted at 30° on the roofs.
- **Materials so far**: limestone (paving), stone, timber, glass, canopies, signage (the `F-*` layers).

### Concept threads already in the design

The skill should build on these, not replace them:

- A shaded pedestrian souq spine.
- Courtyard plazas as the public heart.
- Water, palms and a star geometry as the landscape language.
- Mashrabiya used as a real privacy screen, not as decoration.

### Observations on the current sheet set (2026-10-18)

These are taste observations, not the user's preferences. Raise them when working on the set, and ask before changing anything:

- **Titles**: drawing titles are bright red (ACI red) and switch position, top-left on some sheets and centred under the drawing on others. Pick one position and one quieter colour.
- **Colour**: dimensions and annotations use red, blue and cyan together (ACI rainbow). Keep these technical layers grey or black. The landscape plans (L-101, L-103) and the zoning plan (A-003) use saturated yellow, orange and green fills. Given the user likes 100 Architects, keep the boldness but make it one coherent palette that repeats across all the landscape and diagram sheets, instead of muting it.
- **Scale**: several sheets have a small drawing floating in a large empty field (A-203, A-204, L-104, A-402). Scale the drawing up or pair it with another.
- **Line weights**: walls, furniture and dimensions plot at similar weights on several plans. Separate cut, projection and annotation more clearly.

## Al-Hadbaa Commercial Complex (مجمع الحدباء التجاري)

Not in this repo yet.

### Facts

- **Identity**: a commercial complex in Mosul, named after Al-Hadbaa (the leaning minaret of the Great Mosque of al-Nuri, the city's symbol).
- **Still unknown, to ask the user**: site and area, program, number of floors, client, design stage, existing drawings, and whether it shares a design language with the souq or must look clearly different.

### Note

Al-Hadbaa is the most obvious reference for a Mosul project, and therefore the riskiest. Using the minaret's lean or silhouette as a logo or a facade gesture is direction A (step 2 of `SKILL.md`). Use it only if the user wants it, and then as a principle (verticality, brick bands, the texture of the brick), not a copy.

The two projects must not look alike. Check `design-log.md` whenever designing for either one.
