# Precedents

## Al-Yarmouk Souq: covered market, shaded commercial streets, courtyards

Found 2026-10-18.

### Architecture

| Precedent | Lesson for the souq |
|---|---|
| [The Souk, Abu Dhabi Central Market, by Foster + Partners](https://www.dezeen.com/2011/05/06/the-souk-abu-dhabi-central-market-byfoster-partners/) | The market is a sequence of courtyards and alleys with colonnades. Sliding roofs and walls control the climate and let natural ventilation in. The shade is operable, not fixed. A Foster precedent, so it matches the user's taste. |
| [Souq Waqif, Doha, Aga Khan Award shortlist 2010](https://the.akdn/en/how-we-work/our-agencies/aga-khan-trust-culture/akaa/souk-waqif) | Metal roofs were replaced with traditional timber, bamboo and clay-straw roofs for insulation. It stays a working market and a public space, not a heritage theme park. Lesson: insulated, honestly built shade. Warning: critics call it a "heritage industry" piece, so don't copy its image. |
| [Souk al-Saqatiyya, Aleppo, by the Aga Khan Trust for Culture](https://archnet.org/sites/20464) | A 150 m vaulted souq with 52 shops. Roof, central passage and services were rehabilitated together. Lesson: the roof and the services are one design problem. Won the ICCROM-Sharjah prize, 2020. |
| [Famalicão Municipal Market, by Rui Mendes Ribeiro](https://www.dezeen.com/?p=1707924) | One large metal and glass canopy over the whole market square. Its triangular pattern comes from the old building's turret. Lesson: a single canopy can unify a market, and its pattern can come from something already on site. |
| [Santa Caterina Market, Barcelona, by EMBT](https://www.archdaily.com/82712/video-santa-caterina-market-embt-associated-architects) | An undulating roof with a colourful mosaic of the products sold below. Lesson: bold colour on the roof, the fifth facade seen from above and from neighbouring buildings. It fits the 100 Architects side of the user's taste. |
| [Surat Diamond Bourse, by Morphogenesis](https://www.dezeen.com/2023/07/26/worlds-largest-office-building-morphogenesis-surat-diamond-bourse/) | Courtyards between the wings are shaded by the building itself and used for informal trade all year. Lesson: let the mass of the building make the shade, instead of adding devices to it. |
| [Winding Breeze, Sharjah, by 100 Architects (concept)](https://www.designboom.com/architecture/100architects-plazas-corniche-sharjah-08-18-2022/) | Looping paths enclose small plazas with activities and shade. Lesson: the plaza is a sequence of activity pockets, not one large empty square. Still a proposal, not built. |
| [Shade strategies, ArchDaily](https://www.archdaily.com/1042931/beyond-the-canopy-six-architectural-strategies-for-urban-shade) | OMA's sculptural canopies in LA create shaded outdoor rooms for markets and events. Lesson: shade must work across the whole day of use, not only at noon. |

### Open directions for the souq, built from these lessons

- **B (Foster / Abu Dhabi)**: the 8 m corridors and the pergola plaza get an operable or ventilated roof. Shade and airflow become the main architecture of the souq.
- **C (EMBT / 100 Architects)**: one strong, colourful roof or canopy for the plaza and the fountain court, with its colour taken from the goods or the place, seen from the multi-storey building and the overpass.
- **A, the obvious (avoid)**: arches and a mashrabiya pattern applied to the shop fronts.

### Social media

Real souq or mall campaigns of a high standard were hard to find in the search. Ask the user for accounts they like in this sector.

- [Ads of the World](https://www.adsoftheworld.com/campaigns/arab-national-bank-anb-social-media) and Behance hold regional Arabic campaigns, mostly banking and FMCG. Use them for Arabic type and layout ideas, not as a souq precedent.
- Architecture accounts named in roundups (for example [Dezeen's list](https://www.dezeen.com/?p=1058384)):
  - Bjarke Ingels for captions with wit.
  - John Pawson for a minimal feed with no captions.
  - OFIS for strong context photos.
- Lesson for the souq's account: tell the project through its own drawings and its shade, using the "drawing as graphic" and "detail crop" archetypes in `social.md`, rather than shopping-mall promo templates.
