# Preferences

This is the user's own taste, learned over time. Read it before every design: its firm entries override every default in the skill. Update it whenever the user reacts clearly to a design (SKILL.md, step 8).

## How to record a preference

- **Write it as a concrete rule with its reason**, for example "Prefers brick and Mosul marble tones over white render. Said white boards look like every student project." A vague entry such as "likes minimal" is useless. Ask yourself what exactly they liked.
- **Scope it.** Put it under the right heading: global, a domain (architecture, social, Arabic type) or a specific client or brand. Disliking a blue palette for one client does not mean the user dislikes blue.
- **Mark its strength.**
  - `tentative`: seen once, or inferred from a choice or an edit.
  - `firm`: the user stated it as a general rule, or it showed up in two or more separate reactions.
  - When a tentative entry is confirmed again, change it to firm. Don't add a duplicate.
- **Date it and give the evidence** in a few words: `(2026-10-18, picked direction C over B)`.
- **Newer wins.** When a new reaction contradicts an old entry, replace the old entry and note the change in the history. Never keep both.
- **Keep it short.** Merge overlapping entries. When the file passes about 150 lines, consolidate it.
- **Firm and general preferences** that contradict a default in `architecture.md`, `social.md`, `arabic-type.md` or `SKILL.md` belong in that file: edit the default there and log the edit in the history below.

Entry format:

```
- [firm|tentative] Rule, with its reason. (YYYY-MM-DD, evidence)
```
