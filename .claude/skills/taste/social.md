# Social media

This file applies to every kind of post and account: an architecture office or student portfolio, real estate and project sales, retail and malls, restaurants and cafés, products, services, events, announcements, Ramadan and Eid, educational and explainer posts, recruitment, and personal brands. The method stays the same. The facts, references and archetype mix change with each account.

### Purpose decides the archetype

| Post purpose | Strong archetypes |
|---|---|
| Showing a project or product | Full-bleed image, detail crop, drawing as graphic, process |
| Selling (units, shops, offers) | Number or fact, split, carousel that ends with one clear call to action |
| Announcing (opening, event, date) | Typographic poster |
| Explaining or educating | Carousel built on a diagram |
| Occasions (Ramadan, Eid, national days) | Typographic poster or detail crop in the brand's own system. Never the stock crescent-and-lantern template. |
| Trust (testimonials, team, behind the scenes) | Quote, real photo |

## Generic tells

### Visual

- Purple-to-blue or orange-to-pink gradient backgrounds, blurred blobs, glassmorphism cards
- A centred headline over a stock photo with a dark overlay (the default template look)
- 3D emoji or clay icons, glossy badges, "NEW" ribbons, starbursts
- Drop shadows and outer glows on text
- More than two typefaces, everything bold, everything centred
- Every post using the same template with only the text changed
- AI imagery with plastic skin, extra fingers, impossible structures, or a generic "futuristic city"
- Mockup clichés: a phone floating at an angle with a shadow, a generic hand holding a phone
- Text running edge to edge with no margin

### Copy

- Openers like "In today's fast-paced world", "Are you looking for…", "Unlock", "Elevate", "Discover the magic"
- Arabic clichés: "هل تبحث عن…؟"، "نقدم لكم"، "الأفضل في…"، "بلمسة عصرية"، "تصاميم تلبي جميع احتياجاتكم"، "جودة عالية وأسعار منافسة"
- Runs of emoji (🚀✨🔥💯), a wall of 30 hashtags, or "link in bio 👇" as the main message

## Formats

These are defaults. Check the platform's current specs when exact sizes matter.

- **Instagram feed**: 1080×1350 (4:5) or 1080×1440 (3:4). The profile grid shows a 3:4 crop, so keep key content inside it.
- **Stories, Reels, TikTok**: 1080×1920. Keep text and faces out of the top ~250 px and the bottom ~350 px, where the interface sits. On Reels and TikTok also keep clear of the right edge, where the buttons are.
- **Carousels**: every slide the same size. The first slide is the hook and the last is the payoff or call to action.
- **Facebook and LinkedIn**: 4:5 portrait or 1:1 square. A 4:5 master usually works on both.

## Series system

A series system cures both the generic look and the repetitive one. Define it once per account or campaign:

1. **Grid**: margins (at least 64 px at 1080 wide), columns, and a fixed baseline for the headline.
2. **Type**: one display style and one text style, with sizes in px.
3. **Palette**: from the brand or the subject. Sample it from the account's own material: the building's materials for an architecture office, the food for a restaurant, the product for a product brand.
4. **Signature move**: one recurring device.
5. **Archetypes**: 5–7 layouts the series rotates through (list below).

Every post then uses the same system with a different composition.

### Layout archetypes

1. Full-bleed image, small type in one corner
2. Typographic poster: the type is the image (a big number, one word, one short sentence)
3. Split: image on one half, text on the other. Alternate the sides.
4. Detail crop: a macro of a material, joint or texture, with the context carried by the caption
5. Drawing as graphic: plan, section or elevation linework on a flat colour. This is especially strong for architecture accounts.
6. Process or before/after: sketch, then model, then built
7. Grid of 4–9 small images (a catalogue moment)
8. Number or fact: one big figure with a one-line explanation
9. Quote: a client's or user's own words, with attribution

### Rotation rules

- No two consecutive posts share an archetype.
- Alternate dense posts with calm ones.
- Before publishing, look at the feed as a 3×3 grid. Alternate light with dark and image with type, so the grid has rhythm rather than a checkerboard of identical tiles.

## Readability at phone size

- At 1080 px wide: headlines at least 64 px, body text at least 32 px, nothing smaller than 26 px. Set Arabic 15–20% larger (see `arabic-type.md`).
- Keep a single-image post to 12–15 words. Longer text belongs in the caption or a carousel.
- Text contrast is at least 4.5:1 against its background. Over photos, place text on a calm area of the image or on a solid band, not under a full dark overlay.
- **3-second test**: shrink the post to ~350 px wide. The main message must read within three seconds.

## Copy

- **Hook with a specific**: a number, a place, a material, a problem or a result. "٤٠٠ متر مربع من الظل في قلب السوق" beats "تصميم عصري يجمع بين الأصالة والمعاصرة", and "خبز يطلع من الفرن كل ساعتين" beats "أشهى المخبوزات بأعلى جودة".
- **One message per post**, with one concrete call to action ("احجز موعد", "شوف المشروع كامل"), not "تواصل معنا للمزيد".
- **Register**: write the way the audience speaks. Use MSA for formal brands and Iraqi dialect when the brand speaks that way. Don't mix the two in one post.
- **Hashtags**: 3–5 specific ones, not 30 generic ones.
- **Caption structure**: vary it across posts (question, short story, list, single line). Repetition shows in captions as clearly as in visuals.

## Imagery

- Real photos beat stock, and stock beats AI. An AI image has to survive a close look at hands, text, structure, perspective and local context (signs, cars, clothing and plants that are right for the place).
- Give the whole series one colour grade.
- Crop with intent. A tight crop on a detail is more distinctive than a wide default shot.

## Architecture content on social

- Post the project's own drawings, sections, details, models, site photos and process sketches. They are unique by nature.
- Tell one project as a carousel: context, problem, idea (diagram), drawing, detail, result.
- Don't repost the same hero render. Cut a new detail from it instead.

- [ ] At most two typefaces, and at most three colours plus neutrals
- [ ] Readable at phone size, with safe zones respected
- [ ] No generic tells, visual or copy
